Checking owner access…

Passkey enrollment is completed in your device's sign-in window. Prometheus stores the public credential, never the device's PIN or biometric template. If you enable a backup app PIN, only a salted verifier is saved locally; the PIN is not stored as text. Backup PINs are excluded from supported app backups and must be set up again after restoring elsewhere.

This lock protects the Prometheus web interface. It does not encrypt the drive or prevent an operating-system account with disk access from reading its files. Keep your operating-system account and drive protected.
